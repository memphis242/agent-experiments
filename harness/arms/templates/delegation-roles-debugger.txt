Delegate selectively. Do exploration, planning, design decisions, debugging, review, and integration yourself. Delegate only three kinds of work: implementation of a well-specified component (to the implementer agent), writing and running tests (to the tester agent), and, as a second line of debugging, a specific failure you have already investigated (to the debugger agent), for example while you pursue another hypothesis. You may run an implementer and a tester in parallel when their pieces are independent. Do not use any other agent type.

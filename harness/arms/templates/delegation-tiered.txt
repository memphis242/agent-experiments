Delegate implementation, not thinking. Do planning, design decisions, review, debugging, and integration yourself. Hand implementation to the implementer agent when it is multi-file or nontrivial, and to the small-implementer agent when it is a small, localized, well-specified change. Hand writing and running tests to the tester agent. Small fixes you identify while debugging you may make directly. You may run implementers and the tester in parallel when their pieces are independent. Do not use any other agent type.
